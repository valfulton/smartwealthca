Real Estate License Renewal Course at BRG Realty

By BRG Realty Business & Commercial Team · October 6, 2026 · 3 min read

California real estate licenses must be renewed every four years, and renewal requires completing continuing-education (CE) hours approved by the California Department of Real Estate (DRE). In May 2026, BRG Realty hosted a license renewal course at our San Jose headquarters to help agents get it done together.

Why take CE with BRG?

  • Complete required hours alongside colleagues
  • Ask questions in person
  • Combine renewal with networking at the office
Requirements change. Confirm your current CE requirements and renewal deadline on the California DRE website.
