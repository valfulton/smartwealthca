Introducing BRG Sunset Sessions: Happy-Hour Sails from Emeryville

By BRG Realty Business & Commercial Team · October 6, 2026 · 4 min read
ShareLinkedInFacebookXEmailText

Some of the best conversations happen away from the office. BRG Sunset Sessions is a new series of small-group happy-hour sails on San Francisco Bay, leaving from Emery Cove Yacht Harbor in Emeryville as the sun sets behind the Golden Gate.

Ask us about upcoming events →

A theme for every sail

  • Dealmakers' Sail: business owners thinking about an exit, and buyers looking for their next company
  • Lenders & Investors Sail: SBA and commercial lenders, private capital and 1031 investors
  • Waterfront Property Tour: a look at Emeryville, Oakland, Alameda and Sausalito waterfront property from the water
  • Agents' Appreciation Sail: a thank-you to agents who refer business and commercial clients to BRG
  • Partners' Sail: for our event sponsors and vendors

Why the water?

Our team works with commercial and waterfront property all around the Bay. Seeing the shoreline from the water is the best way to understand it, and a small group on a boat makes for real conversations.

How to join

Each sail holds a small group, so dates go to the interest list first. Join the Sunset Sessions interest list →

Interested in hosting a sail for your clients or sponsoring one? Partner with us.
