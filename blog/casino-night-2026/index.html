NorCal Casino Night at BRG Realty: Friday, October 16, 2026

By BRG Realty Business & Commercial Team · October 6, 2026 · 4 min read
ShareLinkedInFacebookXEmailText

BRG Realty presents NorCal Casino Night on Friday, October 16, 2026, from 6 to 10 PM at BRG headquarters, 1900 Camden Ave, San Jose. Expect blackjack tables, a prize wall and a room full of Bay Area agents, brokers, investors and the clients they bring.

Ask us about upcoming events →

For agents: stack the deck for your clients

Pre-buy chips for past clients, hot leads or your favorite lender. Their chips wait at check-in under their name, and your name goes on the "Hosting Agents" board.

  • Single Stack (1 client): $50
  • Date Night (2 guests): $90
  • Host a Table (6 guests, reserved seating): $250

Early-bird pricing ends Saturday, October 10. Text Val at 530-282-5228 with guest names.

For businesses: sponsor the night

Sponsorship tiers run from a $250+ in-kind prize to the $5,000 Royal Flush presenting sponsorship, with add-ons like the Top Shelf Bar, Photo Booth and Dealer Aprons. See all sponsorship tiers →

Chips are for entertainment only and have no cash value. Prizes are awarded per posted event rules.
