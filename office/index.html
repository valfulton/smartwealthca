Our office · 1900 Camden Ave, San Jose

A San Jose landmark, and home base for BRG

Our business and commercial team works out of BRG Realty's headquarters at 1900 Camden Ave in the Cambrian Park neighborhood of San Jose: a building with four decades of local history and a long run as a gathering place for Silicon Valley entrepreneurs.

History

From 1980s landmark to business hub

  1. The 1980sA building San Jose remembers

    The glass-fronted building at 1900 Camden Ave has been a recognizable stop on Camden Avenue since the 1980s, when much of the surrounding area was still turning from orchards and fields into neighborhoods.

  2. Real estate rootsA South Bay real estate office

    Gregg Bunker, who grew up in the area in a real estate family, built a real estate office here from scratch and grew it into one of the most successful offices in the South Bay.

  3. Next chapterA neighborhood photo shop

    The building later housed a Wolf Camera location offering photo services to the community.

  4. TodaySilicon Valley Business Center and BRG HQ

    Today 1900 Camden Ave is home to BRG Realty's headquarters, the Silicon Valley Business Center, Bunker Wealth Management and Silicon Valley Property Management Group, plus the entrepreneurs and small businesses who work out of the center's private offices, flex desks and conference rooms.
