Selling a business

How a confidential sale works

Every sale follows the same six stages. You'll know what's happening at each one and what we need from you.

  1. 1

    Valuation

    We review three years of financials, recast them to show true owner earnings, and set a price range the market will support.

    Tax returns · P&Ls · add-backs
  2. 2

    Confidential profile

    We write the offering memorandum and a blind teaser that describes the business without naming it.

    Teaser · offering memorandum
  3. 3

    Marketing and screening

    The teaser goes to buyer networks and listing platforms. Buyers sign an NDA and show proof of funds before seeing details.

    NDA · buyer profile · proof of funds
  4. 4

    Offers and LOI

    We compare offers on price, terms, financing and transition, then sign a letter of intent with the strongest buyer.

    Letter of intent
  5. 5

    Due diligence and financing

    The buyer verifies the books, lease and operations while their lender underwrites the loan.

    Diligence list · lender package
  6. 6

    Closing

    Purchase agreement, lease assignment, and escrow. Funds are released and ownership transfers.

    APA · escrow · bill of sale
Commercial loans

Loan programs we arrange

The right structure depends on what you're buying and how much you're putting down. We start with a short pre-qualification conversation.

SBA 7(a)
Buying a business, partner buyouts, working capital, equipmentGood fit when the purchase is mostly goodwill and cash flow, not hard assets
SBA 504
Owner-occupied buildings, major equipmentGood fit when your business will occupy most of the property
Conventional
Investment property and owner-user purchasesGood fit with strong property income or borrower financials
Bridge
Short-term financing before a refinance or saleGood fit when you need to close fast or reposition a property
Construction
Ground-up builds and major renovationsGood fit when plans, budget and permits are in place

Loans are arranged through BRG Realty Corp and made by third-party lenders. Rates, terms and approval are set by the lender and depend on the borrower and property. Nothing on this page is a commitment to lend.
